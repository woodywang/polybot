Four hedging policies, one order book

Every arm reads the same consolidated Binance + Coinbase feed and the same Polymarket book. The only variable is when to buy the opposite side. Equity starts at a notional $1,000 and moves only on settled markets, 2026-09-11 14:50–16:30 UTC.

There is no account balance — three caps set the size

--bankroll 1000Kelly denominator only
--max-usd 25per fill
--max-per-market 60per market & side
--kelly 0.25fraction

The harness never tracks a balance, so the “stake” in the run reports is cumulative capital deployed, not capital at risk at once. The number that would actually have to be funded is peak simultaneous exposure, measured below at $941 – $1,498 depending on the arm.

Equity

Cumulative P&L over settled markets, marked at each settlement. The $1,000 rule is the starting line, not a floor.

Drawdown

Distance below each arm’s own running peak. Under a random walk this envelope widens as σ√n, which is why a fixed percentage stop-loss would fire on ordinary noise here.
